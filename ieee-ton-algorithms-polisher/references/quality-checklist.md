# Quality Checklist

Before finalizing a substantial algorithm-section rewrite, check the following.

## Technical preservation

- All original inputs, outputs, variables, update rules, loops, stopping criteria, and subproblems are preserved or explicitly flagged.
- No new algorithmic operation is introduced without user instruction.
- No convergence, optimality, approximation, or complexity claim is strengthened beyond the draft.
- Pseudocode variables match the prose and the problem formulation.

## Narrative quality

- The algorithm is motivated by a specific feature of the formulated problem.
- The high-level design idea appears before procedural detail.
- The prose explains stages and rationale rather than restating every line.
- The final paragraph connects the algorithm to analysis, implementation, or simulations.

## LaTeX readiness

- LaTeX environments are balanced.
- Captions, labels, citations, line references, and equation references are preserved.
- The output is in a fenced code block when the user asks for LaTeX source.
- The polished text can be pasted into a manuscript with minimal cleanup.
